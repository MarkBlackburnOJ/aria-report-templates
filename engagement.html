Open rate

What “opened” means: the report page loaded in the recipient’s browser and fired its tracking beacon. A report opened on several devices counts once. Email preview panes or blocked images can make a real open go uncounted, so treat the rate as a floor. The KPI cards and every table reflect the active filters, and filters stay applied as you move between tabs; unopened rows are tinted red. Published reports are pruned after a day or two, so only the most recent rows carry a link to the live report — older ones show the store as plain text. Opening a report from here won’t count as an open. Times are SAST. Source: dbo.vReportEngagement.
